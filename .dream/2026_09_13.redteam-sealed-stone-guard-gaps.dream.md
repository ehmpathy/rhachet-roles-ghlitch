# 🌙 dream: a terminal state for a critical third-party disclosure

kind: design · owner: guardian (habitat redteam) · caught 2026-09-13

## .the cue that fired

the i018 l3 arch-defects reviewer (r011) read the stone prose as a state machine and surfaced three
gaps in the skill's `templates/`. two were mechanical and are fixed in the skill
(`3.verify.guard` now blocks an absent severity; `2.detect.stone` grounds on the public canon when
`inventory.of=vuln.class` is absent). one needs a design call, and is owed here.

the templates live under `src/domain.roles/guardian/skills/security.habitat.redteam/templates/`.
they are skill source, editable with no route grant; only a route's own `.stone`/`.guard` files
under `.behavior/` are sealed.

## .the gap, precisely

a `third-party disclosure` claim is never repaired by design (`4.repair.stone`, private channel
only). a **critical** one therefore never leaves "open", so `5.promote`'s convergence test can never
pass, and the budget-out terminal tells the human to "add budget" when more budget cannot close it.

## .the shape of the fix

add a third terminal state to `5.promote.stone` §5.2 (draft name `blocked-on-third-party`): a
critical third-party-disclosure claim routes there at verify time, outside the budget/loop cycle,
and the verdict names the private channel it awaits. guard it in `5.promote.guard`: a critical
third-party claim counted against convergence, or answered with "add budget", is a blocker.

## .why it is not done in this round

the state's name and its effect on the loop (does the loop terminate, or continue on the other
claims?) are a design decision for the wisher, not a mechanical edit.

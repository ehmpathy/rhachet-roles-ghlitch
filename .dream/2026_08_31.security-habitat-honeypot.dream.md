dream =

entool the guardian with **honeypots** — decoy resources that detect interaction, crack (trip) the
moment they are touched, and notify us that they caught a probe.

name proposed: `security.habitat.honeypot` — under the same `security.<subject>.<technique>`
namespace as the redteam sweep. the guardian owns the **habitat** subject (the environment folks
work in); a honeypot is a habitat ward, so it sits beside `security.habitat.redteam`. a
`security.product.honeypot` twin may follow on the hardener side, by the seam.

## why — the nazar made operational

the guardian mark is the nazar (the evil-eye ward). folk belief holds a nazar cracks when it absorbs
an evil eye meant for you: a decoy that takes the strike and, by the crack, tells you it was struck.
a honeypot is exactly that, made operational — a ward that catches, cracks, and notifies. this
extends the guardian from a passive cited inventory into the vision active-watchguard north star.

## scope ideas

- canary tokens and canary files — a decoy secret or doc that alerts on any access
- decoy credentials — fake creds that notify the instant they are used
- decoy endpoints tied to attack.vector=wifi.public — a decoy captive-portal or SSID that trips on a
  probe
- a notify channel — the crack maps to an alert (the tripwire signal)

## fit

- complements the planned answer-router follow-on and the redteam sweep
  (`security.habitat.redteam`) — a redteam finds the holes, a honeypot watches them
- lives under src/domain.roles/guardian/skills/ (empty today)
- honors the guardian posture: it wards, it does not attack; it watches, and stays humble

## the shape to imagine

the same `init` route-backed shape as the redteam sweep:

```
rhx security.habitat.honeypot init --kind canary-token|canary-file|decoy-cred
rhx security.habitat.honeypot --mode plan
rhx security.habitat.honeypot --mode apply
```

## acceptance

- a skill (or skills) that deploys a honeypot and emits a notification when tripped
- cited and documented per the guardian citation discipline
- integration-tested (a trip fires a notify)

## .the gap, precisely

the multi-cycle convergence / re-detect trust model carries open `PENDING-REDETECT` keys forward in
`$route/.claims/0.cycle.md` across a rewind, so the next detect re-confirms each repair regardless of
what the rewind does to the per-stone `.yield.md` files. that design is **defensive by construction** —
it assumes the worst (the rewind may or may not archive the `.claims/` yields) and routes around it.

what is NOT checked: how the framework's rewind archiver actually treats a gitignored sub-path under
`$route/`. the framework archives a stone's yield at `$route/<stone>.yield.md`; this skill deliberately
writes to `$route/.claims/<stone>.yield.md` (the gitignore override). whether the generic archiver
reaches INTO `.claims/` on a rewind is unverified against the real `route.drive`.

- checkable evidence today: `5.promote.stone` line ~60 ("⚠️ open risk — the rewind may NOT archive
  `.claims/` yields") and `2.detect.stone` §2.0 (the carried-key re-hunt). both cite the uncertainty;
  neither proves the behavior.

## .the shape of the fix

a one-time spike (not a committed unit test — it needs the live framework, not a temp git habitat):

1. scaffold a real redteam route, bind it, drive to `2.detect` with a claim yield in `.claims/`.
2. drop a sentinel into `$route/.claims/2.detect.yield.md` and into `$route/.claims/0.cycle.md`.
3. run `rhx route.stone.set --stone 2.detect --as rewound`.
4. observe: does the archiver move/clear/rename either `.claims/` file, or leave it untouched?

record the observed behavior in the stone prose (replace the "unverified" hedge with the result),
and — if the archiver DOES reach into `.claims/` — confirm the `0.cycle.md` carry survives or adjust
the carry target to a path the archiver leaves alone.

## .why it is not done in this round

SAFE: no — it touches the live `route.drive` framework, outside this skill's own surface, so it is
not a change that lands in the diff already open. CLEAN: no — it needs a driven route against the real
framework (a genTempDir habitat cannot stand in for it), so it ripples beyond this branch. the
defensive carry already routes around a wrong assumption, so a release without the spike is safe; the
spike upgrades a hedge to a fact, it does not unblock the loop. ⇒ deferred as a follow-up, and the
carry design holds the line meanwhile.
